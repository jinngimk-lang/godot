## What this closes

This means the next anatomically credible support pose no longer has to be regenerated by a solver or embedded as opaque procedural code. Once a pose passes Macro/Meso, it can be frozen and reused for cup/bottle adaptation and later GLB export.

## Current primary-source research boundary

Fresh MPFB source/doc review established:

- MPFB supports reusable full/partial pose authoring/loading workflows;
- MPFB also exposes `AnimationService.import_bvh_file_as_pose`, but the implementation explicitly warns that it destructively copies source BVH bone-roll values into the destination rig and will ruin the destination rig's original roll values;
- therefore an external MakeHuman BVH must never be applied directly to the production GameEngine hero rig;
- permissively licensed external poses may be loaded only into a sacrificial/default staging rig as anatomical/silhouette reference;
- the final GameEngine support pose must be manually/keyframe corrected and then saved through the non-destructive same-rig partial pose asset path.

A MakeHuman Community CC0 pose pack contains an object-holding / wine-glass pose that may be useful strictly as anatomical reference. It is not assumed to be compatible or visually correct without evidence.
